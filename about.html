About Braai Masters

Traditional South African food prepared with passion, flavour and quality.

Our Story

Braai Masters is a South African food business inspired by the traditional braai and chesa nyama experience.

The name Braai Masters came from the experience of grilling meat over a braai stand. The flames, delicious smell of spices and the taste of freshly prepared meat attracted customers and created a memorable food experience.

Customers would often return and ask for the "Braai Master". This helped inspire the name Braai Masters.

What We Do

Traditional Food

We prepare traditional South African meals using a variety of proteins, starches and vegetable sides.

Catering Services

Braai Masters provides catering services for family gatherings, weddings, community events and year-end functions.

Quality Food

Our meals are prepared using carefully selected ingredients and traditional cooking methods.

Community

The business aims to create employment opportunities and transfer valuable skills to people interested in the food industry.

Our Vision

Our vision is to grow Braai Masters into a recognised food and catering business with branches in different locations locally and internationally.

We aim to introduce tourists and visitors to the delicious taste of South African and African traditional food while continuing to serve local customers.
